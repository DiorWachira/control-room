Control Room
============

Live site: https://diorwachira.github.io/control-room/
Source:    https://github.com/DiorWachira/control-room

Control Room is the on-call desk for my live apps. Every 15 minutes it probes seven of my
deployed projects (Resolve, RepoLens, Sunrise Showtime, Get2theMoney, Seed Mind, MetaMorph and
Context Profiler) and gives each one an error budget. A burn-rate engine, built on the Google SRE
Workbook's multi-window alerts, watches how fast each budget is being spent. When a budget burns
too fast, it opens an incident, pages me on Telegram and keeps a timeline. When the incident
closes, it drafts a blameless postmortem. A deploy gate blocks risky releases while a budget is
under its floor.

The site has three screens, styled as a 1960s mission control room:
  - Status:   the live public board, with one lamp per app and a 91-day history strip.
  - Budgets:  a console wall that replays a real outage from 8 November minute by minute
              (draining budget tanks, burn meters, master caution, the deploy-gate switch).
  - RSV-1042: that outage's incident page: SLA clock, burn trace, timeline and postmortem draft.

First visit? Take the guided tour from the invitation, or press ? anywhere for the help drawer.
